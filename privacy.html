Bolt Bots Privacy Policy

Effective 5 November 2026 · Published by Wraplet · Contact: info@wraplet.app

Short version: Bolt Bots has no accounts, no sign-in and no in-app purchases. Your game progress stays on your phone. The game is free and shows ads from Google AdMob, which collects some device data to show and measure ads, as described below.

1. Who we are

Bolt Bots ("the game") is a puzzle game for Android published by Wraplet ("we", "us"). If you have any question about this policy or your data, email info@wraplet.app.

2. Data the game itself stores

The game saves your progress (levels, stars, coins, gems, boosters, bots, settings and reward-video progress) only on your device, in the app's private storage. We do not run servers for the game, we do not receive this data, and we cannot see it. Uninstalling the game or using "Reset progress" in Settings deletes it.

The game does not ask for your name, email address, contacts, photos, location permission or any account.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game is free because it shows ads: optional reward videos you choose to watch, and occasional ad breaks between levels (you can switch ad breaks off for a day by watching reward videos). Ads are provided by Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. To show, personalise (where you allow it), measure ads and prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process:

DataPurpose
Device identifiers, such as the Android advertising IDAdvertising, analytics, fraud prevention
IP address and approximate location derived from itAd delivery, fraud prevention
App interactions with ads (views, clicks, video completion)Ad measurement and rewards
Diagnostics (crash and performance data of the ads SDK)Keeping ads working

This data is sent to Google and handled under Google's Privacy Policy. Learn how Google uses information from apps that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. Data is encrypted in transit.

4. Your choices

  • Consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland): before any ads load, the game shows Google's consent form where the law requires it. You can change your choice later from Settings > Privacy in the game.
  • Personalised ads: you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalisation, in your Android settings (Settings > Google > Ads, or Settings > Privacy > Ads).
  • Deleting data: uninstall the game or use "Reset progress" to delete game data on your device. For data held by Google, use your Google account and device ad settings.

5. Children

Bolt Bots is intended for players aged 13 and older and is not directed at children under 13. Ads are limited to content rated for teens. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has used the game in a way that shared personal data, contact us and we will help.

6. Purchases

There are no in-app purchases. Coins, gems, boosters and skins are earned by playing or by choosing to watch reward videos.

7. Security and retention

Game data stays on your device until you delete it. Data processed by Google AdMob is retained under Google's policies.

8. Changes

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the effective date above.
